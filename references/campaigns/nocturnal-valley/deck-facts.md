## The partner case — slides 5 and 8

Four arguments, deck wording: 24/7 onsite immersion versus one-day events; attendees "actively embrace brands that enhance their festival experience"; a content-driven environment (user-generated content, social sharing, influencer amplification, artist collaboration); and the emerging-festival argument — "Early partners gain prime positioning and preferred pricing as the festival scales year over year." Slide 8 adds multi-day product sampling to "a captive, receptive audience."

The early-partner line is the one deck argument aimed specifically at a first-edition objection, which makes it the deck's most useful sentence for outreach.
